## Clash of Cards: Battle of Realms

Welcome to *Clash of Cards: Battle of Realms*! 🎮 This project is a strategic card game developed in Unity, inspired by the mechanics of Gwent, with a unique twist featuring characters and elements from the popular Clash of Clans and Clash Royale universes.

### Features:
- **Dynamic Decks**: Choose from a variety of cards inspired by Clash of Clans and Clash Royale.
- **Strategic Gameplay**: Plan your moves, counter your opponent's strategies, and lead your deck to victory.
- **Rich Visuals**: Experience a visually engaging interface that brings your favorite Clash characters to life.

### Project Goals:
- Implement card-based combat mechanics similar to Gwent.
- Create a balanced and competitive game environment.
- Ensure a clean, maintainable codebase following best practices in Unity and C#.

### Tech Stack:
- **Unity**: Game engine for 2D development.
- **C#**: Primary programming language.
- **GitHub**: Version control and collaboration platform.

Join us on this epic journey as we combine the worlds of Clash of Clans and Clash Royale into an exciting and challenging card game experience!
